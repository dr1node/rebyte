# ReByte Brand Guidelines

## Overview

ReByte is a web-based collection of online tools.

The ReByte name, logo, visual identity, and official website are identifiers of the original project and must not be used to misrepresent the origin of another product.

## Name and Logo

Unless permission is granted by the relevant rights holder:

- Do not use the ReByte name or logo as the name or logo of another product.
- Do not present a modified or independently operated project as the official ReByte website.
- Do not imply that ReByte endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with another project.
- Do not remove ownership notices from original ReByte brand assets.

## Forks and Modified Projects

Any independently authorized derivative or alternative implementation should use its own distinct name, logo, and visual identity.

Do not use branding or presentation that falsely suggests that an independently operated project is the official ReByte service.

Permission to use source code does not automatically grant permission to use ReByte's brand assets.

## Attribution

When referencing the original project, identify ReByte as the original project and provide a link to the official repository or website where appropriate.

Attribution must not imply that the ReByte maintainer created or endorses modifications made by others.

## Third-Party Assets

Logos, fonts, icons, libraries, and other assets belonging to third parties remain subject to their respective rights and licenses.

## Permission Requests

For questions or requests regarding ReByte branding, contact the project maintainer.

## Disclaimer

This document describes the project's branding expectations. It does not replace applicable copyright, trademark, or other laws.
